## Generation prompt

Use this prompt for a new candidate. For a close revision, also supply
`content/ash-cover.png` as the reference image and specify the intended change.
Review the result against the selected asset before replacing it.

```text
Create a portrait book-cover illustration with a 2:3 aspect ratio, inspired
by the Bulgarian saying "змия под ясен не спи и ясенова сянка не гони".
Use the saying as visual inspiration only; do not print it in the image.

Show one mature European ash tree on the left, one coiled snake in the lower
right foreground, and one compound ash leaf gently drifting down through
the open space above the snake. Include a restrained rural landscape with
sparse ground vegetation and generous open sky.

The ash tree casts a clearly bounded shadow across the ground. The snake's
ENTIRE body and tail lie outside that shadow. Leave a visible patch of light
ground between the shadow's edge and the snake. The snake has one continuous
body, one head and one tapering tail, with plausible coils and fine scales.
Its head rests directly on top of its coils, with no upright neck. Its mouth
is closed. A heavy eyelid leaves the visible eye slightly open, with the
pupil looking upward toward the drifting leaf. It is still sleepy, but wary
of the leaf: quietly watchful, with mild unease rather than aggression.

Make the airborne leaf recognisably European ash: one central stalk with
four pairs of pointed, serrated leaflets and one terminal leaflet. Bend the
central stalk visibly into a loose crescent or shallow S curve. Let the
leaflets curl, droop and twist at slightly different angles. The whole leaf
feels supple and buoyant as it gently floats down. Its terminal leaflet
curls toward the snake's resting head. Avoid a straight rigid axis or an
arrow-like silhouette. Keep open air below it and use only a few delicate
curved drift marks, if needed.

Use dark ink on warm ivory paper, with fine engraved linework, restrained
crosshatching, detailed tree bark and snake scales. Match a quiet book
engraving with a small note of affectionate humour. Keep all three subjects
legible at book-page size and preserve the space around the falling leaf.

No lettering, title, quotation, logo, watermark, frame or decorative border.
No additional animals, people, buildings, magical glow, menace, speech
balloons or sleep symbols. Do not place the snake under the tree's shadow.
Do not substitute a maple-shaped leaf or a stiff botanical arrow.
Deliver the illustration itself, not a photograph or mockup of a book.
```

After generation, inspect the snake's supported head and wary eye, its
separation from the shadow, and the leaf's bend and floating pose. Confirm
that its compound structure remains recognisable and that the image contains
no lettering or added focal subjects.
