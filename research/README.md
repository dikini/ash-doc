# Research

This root contains evaluations of sources, teaching methods, structures,
algorithms, application domains, and other candidate contributions. Research
informs planning; it is not published instruction and does not claim executable
Ash behavior.

Create a focused record at `research/<source-or-question>.md`. Record:

- the question the evaluation answers;
- the source, authors, edition, and material consulted;
- the contribution that could transfer to ash-doc;
- whether the result is inspiration or direct adaptation;
- licensing and redistribution constraints;
- Ash-specific departures that would be required; and
- what should deliberately remain outside the documentation stream.

Research may propose a content unit when the contribution, reader outcome, and
adaptation boundary are clear. It may propose a [`lab/`](../lab/README.md) case
when code must test an Ash design boundary. The resulting plan or experiment
owns its own claims; the research record remains source and method evidence.

Follow the [writing skill](../.agents/skills/writing-ash-documentation/SKILL.md)
for source integrity and adaptation style.

## Teaching references

- [*How to Design Programs*](how-to-design-programs-recursive-algorithms.md)
  contributes design from data, abstraction, termination, and cost reasoning.
- [Liquid Shplait](liquid-shplait-teaching.md) contributes explanations of
  contracts, checking responsibilities, rejections, and guarantee boundaries.
- [*A Data-Centric Introduction to Computing*](data-centric-introduction-to-computing.md)
  contributes meaningful data, development from examples, prediction, and
  revision through small distinguishing cases.
- [Sequencing traditions](sequencing-three-traditions.md) contributes the
  content sequencing of *How to Design Programs*, SICP, and Wirth's
  *Algorithms + Data Structures = Programs* for the Learn extension.
